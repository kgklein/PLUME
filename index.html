PLUME

Plasma in a Linear Uniform Magnetized Environment

PLUME is a Fortran solver for linear electromagnetic dispersion relations in homogeneous, nonrelativistic, magnetized plasmas, supporting kinetic bi-Maxwellian and cold-fluid populations.
