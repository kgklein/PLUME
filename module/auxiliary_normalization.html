Auxiliary thermal adapter, validated in Stage 3B.1 and integrated in 3B.2. Native d_ref inputs retain the physical reference mass/charge/density. No plasma globals, reference temperature, or reference anisotropy are read.

public subroutine make_auxiliary_scale(kpar_d, kperp_d, va_ref_c, b_star, scale, ierr)

The isotropic auxiliary scale has alpha_*=1. The output is unusable on failure. This conversion does not assess the range of all subsequent legacy kinetic intermediate products; see the conditioning report.
