public subroutine calc_kinetic_moments(species, w, kpar_d, kperp_d, a, e, chi, velocity, density, ierr)

Existing calc_eigen current/continuity method in physical coordinates. F=v+audensity*z; removing the density current is essential for drift. This is algebraically its original longitudinal elimination, without intermediate removable Doppler divisions. No kinetic closure is changed.
