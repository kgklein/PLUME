What is calculated

heating_convention='corrected' is the default, independently of method, Bessel mode, and input normalization. It supports collisionless kinetic, cold, and mixed plasmas, including cold references, at single-wavevector diagnostic calls and serial/2D wavevector scans. Collisionless, all-kinetic rho_ref runs also support serial and 2D plasma-parameter scans, with the current plasma held fixed for each frequency derivative. See scan semantics. Each warm population retains its full species absorption/emission diagnostic and the original seven selected channels. Every physical species, including cold ones, contributes to formal wave energy.

These are weak-mode linear response diagnostics, not dimensional thermodynamic heating power, entropy production or an exact conserved energy for arbitrary unstable waves. Cold-fluid reversible energy exchange is not kinetic heating. Cold species' and seven channels are NaN; a fully cold run has no available kinetic-heating total. This differs physically from a calculated zero for a warm population at a nonabsorbing point.

Derivatives, convergence and isolation

Kinetic energy derivatives use centered differences of and Richardson extrapolation. For a species let , fixed /plasma, and :

The implementation tries 13 halving relative steps from to , requiring successive estimates to resolve both matrix Frobenius norm and the -contracted scalar at tolerance , with comparison scales . It selects the best stable window, not necessarily the smallest step. Exported dmatrix_s, dproject_s and dstep_s describe that window; uncertainty is an empirical numerical resolution estimate, not a rigorous error bound. Poles, Bessel/ errors and cancellation can defeat convergence. No fallback silently substitutes the historical normalizer.

Cold derivatives use the analytic differentiation of the normalized fluid momentum/continuity response. Exact real-frequency poles remain failures. The finite-difference sampling of kinetic species does not unnecessarily evaluate cold responses at off-center frequencies. Corrected diagnostic work is local: accepted-mode susc, susc_low, lam and dispersion status remain at the converged mode. Failed energy/heating does not invalidate root, field or species moments.

Availability, applicability and statuses

Corrected diagnostics require collision_type=0. They reject finite-rate physics rather than making new collisional claims. Legacy all-kinetic collisional behavior can be requested explicitly, with known limitations.

Failures include , real cold poles, nonfinite response, unresolved derivative, unresolved and unavailable field. A valid complex dispersion root may therefore have unavailable real-frequency energy/heating. A cold singularity at is not concealed by the mode's finite imaginary part.

Applicability bit 1 is set for . Bit 2 flags weak-balance or near-reactive inconsistency. Formal energy can remain resolved (energy_status=0) while an applicability bit makes normalized warm /channels NaN. Raw and terms remain informative but must not be presented as validated thermodynamic heating. A reactive mode is not repaired by dividing by an artificial floor. Numerical energy status and physical applicability are separate fields; the output status tables list both.

Legacy convention

heating_convention='legacy' preserves historical all-kinetic calc_eigen arithmetic, old normalizer (without vacuum electric energy), one-sided derivative, shifted harmonic snapshots and legacy scratch/output behavior. It does not silently inherit corrected mathematics, and cold/mixed legacy heating is not available. It is for reproducibility, not a second accurate complete energy convention.
