Requests, failure isolation and examples

Serial scans use eigen=.true. in the relevant scan_input_N; two-dimensional grids use scan_input_2 for eigen/heating flags. Direct option=-1 attempts available diagnostics at the supplied frequency. option=1 with nscan=0 does not write a standalone eigenfunction table. Map eigen/tensor requests are ignored with warnings, not converted into diagnostic files.

Cold-containing dispatch calculates fields, then each species' warm or cold moment. A failed species moment leaves that block NaN without overwriting valid or other species. A failed field leaves all associated moments unavailable.

Moment statuses:

  • 0 valid;
  • 1 input/classification;
  • 2 zero ;
  • 3 cold Doppler pole;
  • 4 cold cyclotron pole;
  • 5 nonfinite arithmetic;
  • 6 absent population.

Diagnostics must not contaminate subsequent scan points. Corrected heating has separately isolated state and cannot invalidate accepted roots/moments on failure.
