Examples: a twelve-lesson curriculum

Manual home · Quick Start · Modes

All commands below assume the repository root, a completed make -j2, and mkdir -p data/example. Links point to existing input sources; no new input files are invented by this manual. Match each reader to the file's species count and flags. Benchmarks below are current locally verified printed values or explicitly labeled developer references, not universal dispersion relations. A process finishing is not by itself validation of every mode/diagnostic.

Lessons 8 and 9 share a logarithmic scan from to , at fixed . The input requests 400 output steps and 32 substeps per output step for each of two branches, with eigenfunctions and corrected heating enabled. This is a longer continuation example than the bounded single-branch inputs above; inspect failed points and diagnostic statuses throughout the scan.

Lesson 10 scans from to and from 1 to 10, with corrected heating and eigenfunctions. Its 200 by 30 output intervals produce a grid, or 6,231 rows per branch. The .energy companion records exact parameter states in comments. Valid roots can have unavailable heating; inspect the companion statuses and warning log throughout the grid.

Energy is only a formal weak-mode diagnostic; check energy_status and applicability in the companion before interpreting normalized absorption. Lesson 11's ordinary cold example has a small electric fraction. The separate test-only moderate-a, artificial mass-ratio-4 case has f_E about .500000281; do not attribute that number to the supplied physical-mass-ratio example. Creating its explicit public input is later curated-example work.

The field-only warm/cold mixed files used by the Quick Start are simpler companions to lessons 5/6, not new physics examples. They are useful when you want E/B/moments without introducing heating/energy interpretation.

Secondary numerical and historical examples

  • adaptive_bessel.in and faddeeva_accuracy.in are numerical stress/convergence exercises, not beginner benchmarks. Examine harmonic counts, cap warnings and tensor accuracy as described in Numerics.
  • mixed_scan_warm_reference.in and fully_cold_scan.in are existing serial continuation examples with no failed points in this replay.
  • mixed_scan_cold_reference.in finishes but has a failed written point in current replay. Study NaN/recovery deliberately; use mixed_fields_cold_reference.in for an all-success bounded first scan instead.
  • example_map_par.in is an existing linear-map lesson; examine sampled determinants versus refined candidates and branch slots. It is larger than the Quick Start.
  • example_guess_double.in is a large historical 2D scan; the previous audit interrupted it at its short runtime limit. It is not certified as a small, all-success new-user example.
  • Historical radial/parameter/FPC fixtures retain their specialized limitations. No new curated radial/FPC input or scientific extension is claimed here.

Further example work will automate benchmarks and document branch identity. Existing inputs are not rewritten by this manual task. Current execution evidence and all linked-input existence checks are recorded in the manual report.
