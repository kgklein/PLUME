User Manual

PLUME solves linear electromagnetic dispersion relations in homogeneous, nonrelativistic magnetized plasmas, with drifting kinetic bi-Maxwellian and collisionless cold-fluid populations. This manual documents the physical conventions, supported calculations, numerical methods, output diagnostics and limitations of the solver.

For a first calculation, start with installation and the quick start.

Authors, scientific references and citation

PLUME is maintained by Kristopher Klein (kgklein@arizona.edu), Gregory Howes (gregory-howes@uiowa.edu), and Collin Brown (collin.crbrown@gmail.com). The kinetic solver descends from Quataert (1998) and Gregory Howes's Fortran adaptation. Its kinetic equations follow Stix, Waves in Plasmas, Chapter 10, equations 66–73. The cold drifting response follows Verscharen & Chandran (2013), Section 2.1. The selected kinetic heating channels are discussed in the PLUME code paper and Huang et al. (2024).

For publications, cite the PLUME code paper, acknowledge the repository, and record the source revision and numerical settings. The archived code-release citation is:
