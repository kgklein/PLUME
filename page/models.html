Plasma species, models and capabilities

Manual home · Normalization · Modes and scans

PLUME supports bi-Maxwellian and cold populations in mixtures, subject to the restrictions below. If you use one or more cold populations, you must use the d_ref normalization.

Kinetic populations

We define a warm population as a drifting gyrotropic bi-Maxwellian with number density , parallel drift and thermal speeds , . Its equilibrium distribution is

The magnetic field and drift are oriented along z. Kinetic anisotropy . In d_ref, =tauS>0 sets the population to be warm, while tauS=0 sets the population to be cold.
In rho_ref tauS is the reference-to-species temperature ratio and must be positive. No threshold switches a sufficiently narrow Maxwellian to cold fluid. Multiple components of the same particle species may be separated into individual componets, with each population having separate temperatures, densities and drifts.

Cold-fluid populations

Under d_ref, setting tauS=0 selects a cold fluid model. Negative tauS is invalid. A cold population has zero thermal pressure, and thus alphS is physically undefined and ignored by its response. Supply it with 1 as a placeholder.

Cold species retain finite inertia, signed cyclotron frequency, density, charge and parallel drift. Their response includes oblique drift coupling, not just the stationary Stix transverse terms. The response supports complex frequencies, fully cold systems and a cold reference population.

Cold populations have fluid density/velocity eigenfunctions when the Ex=1 field solution and response are valid. Kinetic heating, kinetic harmonic heating and FPC are unavailable for cold species, and report NaN. Their reversible response does, however, contribute to formal wave energy.

Mixtures and species ordering

PLUME stores ordinary susceptibility separately for each population. The dielectric response is , adding the vacuum identity and forming the electromagnetic wave matrix. The full plasma determines the common field polarization, with moments and kinetic absorption use that field with the appropriate per-species response. See Eigenfunctions and Heating for the full expressions.

Species_1 remains the physical reference even if cold. Cold-reference/warm mixtures use the internal auxiliary scale described in Normalization, so there is no need to reorder species merely to obtain a warm reference. Interleaved warm/cold species and several warm populations are supported, subject to the capability below.

Equilibrium checks are warnings

With the symbols defined in Normalization, charge and current imbalance are proportional to and , respectively. Input validation warns at absolute magnitude at least 1e-6. A drifting reference also produces a warning. None of these checks changes density, drift, species order or reference frame, and neither charge neutrality nor zero current is mandatory. Some nonstandard homogeneous equilibria require external charge, currents or forces to maintain the assumed background. Users must make that equilibrium interpretation explicit.
