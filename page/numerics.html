Tensors and the determinant

For Fourier convention exp(i k.r-i omega t), B0 along z and k=(kperp,0,kpar), PLUME stores the ordinary dimensionless per-population tensor susc(species,row,column)=chi_s. With w=omega/Omega_R, kappa=k d_R and a=v_A,R/c, it forms

The tensor n n^T has ordinary transpose, not conjugation; complex n is allowed. Vacuum identity and electromagnetic terms appear exactly once, outside species kernels. Tensor component order is xx,xy,xz; yx,yy,yz; zx,zy,zz. The gyrotropic response has chi_yx=-chi_xy, chi_zx=chi_xz, chi_zy=-chi_yz, without conjugation at complex frequencies. Hermitian symmetry at a real nondissipative point is a separate physical statement.

The API disp and map files use the raw determinant. Direct stdout historically multiplies it by solver_vtp^6 for a warm reference, whereas cold-reference direct evaluation reports it raw. This display scaling does not redefine chi, epsilon or Lambda. Historical comments about multiplying expressions by w^2 do not describe the implemented matrix.

A small determinant can result from scaling or cancellation. Check the normalized determinant, matrix/nullvector residual, finiteness and root stability under controlled resolution changes. A small raw determinant alone does not establish frequency accuracy.

Kinetic response and normalization adapter

The thermal susceptibility follows Stix, Waves in Plasmas, Chapter 10, equations 66–73, for the bi-Maxwellian model. Warm-reference d_ref inputs are converted to the historical thermal coordinates with the reference anisotropy factor. Cold-reference mixtures use the independent auxiliary state, with physical species anisotropies untouched. Physical prefactors, Doppler shifts and thermal/Bessel arguments are invariant under that coordinate choice. The adapter reuses the shared kinetic expressions. Fully cold response bypasses thermal state.

It chooses a species-specific N satisfying both bounds at bessel_tol (default 1e-10), subject to bessel_nmax (default 512). Bounds are evaluated stably in logarithmic form, avoiding subtraction of nearly converged sums from one and premature termination for broad distributions. For lambda=0 the selector handles the weights analytically; supported exact parallel response uses its analytic harmonic limit. This does not enable the currently unsupported all-kinetic kperp=0 dispatcher.

The tail tolerance does not guarantee total susceptibility or root accuracy. Resonance amplification, cancellation, Bessel coefficient accuracy and Z accuracy also matter. Compare all relevant tensor elements and mode frequency while tightening bessel_tol and increasing the cap, especially for broad lambda distributions.

If the cap is reached before the bounds meet tolerance, PLUME warns once for the unchanged request and continues with the maximum allowed N. The .bessel metadata records the convergence as unresolved. A nonfinite Bessel calculation is numerical failure, distinct from a finite but under-resolved sum. There is no silent physical substitution or damping.

The per-species cache tracks physical lambda, request, active N, allocation capacity, coefficients, tail bounds and convergence. Frequency, parallel wavevector, drift and density alone do not require new physical Bessel coefficients. Perpendicular wavevector, temperature, anisotropy, mass or charge changes that alter lambda do. Exact computed-argument matching avoids unbounded approximate reuse; roundoff in forming an equivalent argument can cause a miss, not reuse of incorrect data. Allocation grows as needed without shrinking at every small step. Species-count/order changes cannot reuse the wrong argument.

bessel_mode='fixed' uses bessel_nfixed; 'fixed128' requires nfixed=128. The historical fixed-128 compiled arithmetic and its old cache path are retained for exact compatibility. The historical path does not report per-species cache statistics. Changing the auxiliary scale may require recomputation. Independent choices of Z/heating conventions are required for legacy reproduction.

FPC distributions use a separate internal fixed nbesmax=15, not this adaptive susceptibility policy. Neither bessel_tol nor an adaptive root calculation is evidence that a velocity-space FPC reconstruction is harmonically converged.

The signed parallel convention used by the production zet_in(kpar,zeta) interface is Z(zeta) for kpar>0 and -Z(-zeta) for kpar<0. It is not naive lower-half-plane conjugation.

z_backend='faddeeva' (default) uses bundled Steven G. Johnson code with a C99 bridge and Fortran ISO_C_BINDING. The bridge uses upstream evaluation at machine precision; there is no user Z-tolerance setting, and no Python runtime is required. Its continued fraction/Algorithm-916/Taylor branches are numerical techniques, not changes to the Landau prescription. The historical radius-three approximation error motivated replacement; the audit isolated its susceptibility amplification independently.

The Faddeeva backend assigns a result on every call. Invalid/nonfinite arguments, unsupported kpar=0 calls, arithmetic failures and nonrepresentable outputs propagate invalid status/NaNs through guarded dispersion paths. It does not clamp Z, add imaginary frequency or fall back silently to the old algorithm. Some lower-half-plane Z values exceed binary64 range; this is distinct from an implementation failure. Subsequent regular evaluations must still succeed. Fully cold dispersion does not call Z.

z_backend='legacy' preserves historical zetout arithmetic/stopping behavior, including known accuracy and unassigned-result limitations; it is not an accurate fallback. The Faddeeva bridge uses c_double/c_double_complex matching the promoted Fortran real/complex storage. Under these flags, do not assume nrtype::DP is interchangeable with these C kinds. Provenance and license records the upstream mirror commit, checksums, MIT attribution and unchanged numerical sources. No external shared third-party library is required.

Drifting cold susceptibility

For one species let delta=w-kappa_parallel u, b=kappa_perp u, g=mu/Q signed and A=D mu/(Q^2 a^2), using the physical normalization definitions. The species circular and coupling terms from Verscharen & Chandran, Section 2.1 are

D_H is the Hall coefficient, not the input density ratio D. These are ordinary species chi terms with no vacuum identity. PLUME uses factored denominators (delta-g)(delta+g) and combined transverse formulas to reduce subtractive cancellation. Finite electron mass is retained. At drift zero the cross couplings vanish; parallel stationary transverse roots recover n^2=R or L for R=1+sum R_s, L=1+sum L_s. Perpendicular stationary O/X relations are n^2=P and n^2=RL/S with the total dielectric coefficients, away from poles.

Exact w=0, delta=0 and delta=+/-g are explicitly classified for nonzero density; zero density gives zero susceptibility. A full tensor may be invalid at a pole even if some individual component has a removable cancellation. No pressure, thermal correction, collision floor or artificial damping is added.

Root solving, derivatives and numerical checks

The production root method is a complex secant iteration, with internal tolerance 1e-13, nearby initial fractional offsets 1e-7 and a maximum of 75 iterations. These constants are not user inputs. See Modes for map minima, deduplication/filter order and continuation. Guarded scan points also check status, finiteness and a determinant divided by the cube of the maximum matrix magnitude against 1e-8. This is a scaling/acceptance safeguard, not a universal frequency error estimate or a new branch tracker.

Corrected energy derivatives use centered finite differences with Richardson extrapolation and convergence checks on matrices and their field contractions, together with analytic cold response derivatives. Their physical meaning and resolution limits are on Heating. Failed supplementary energy must not change accepted-mode matrix state or root continuation.

For publishable calculations, vary map resolution or supplied guesses, scan step size, Bessel tolerance/cap and backend. Reconstruct Lambda from species tensors and check field/current identities where relevant. Compare forward/reverse traversal with attention to physical mode crossings. Retain unresolved cap warnings, invalid status and unavailable diagnostic distinctions when analyzing or plotting results.
