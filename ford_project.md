# Plasma in a Linear Uniform Magnetized Environment

<img src="./page/assets/PLUME_logo.png" alt="PLUME logo" width="200"/>
<img src="./page/assets/qrcode_plume_github.png" alt="Repository QR code" width="200"/>


PLUME is a Fortran solver for linear electromagnetic dispersion
relations in homogeneous, nonrelativistic, magnetized plasmas,
supporting kinetic bi-Maxwellian and cold-fluid populations.

**[User Manual](./page/index.html)** |
